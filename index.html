KILLCHAIN
An air-war simulation inspired by the India–Pakistan air war of 6–10 May 2025
LIVE: AI vs AI battle running behind this menu

A simplified, unofficial game. Performance figures are open-source estimates; results come from the simulation each time you play, not from either side's claims. Not a historical record.

HOW TO PLAY
  1. Detect. Radar finds enemies. AEW&C aircraft (the circles with a bar) and ground radars see furthest. Fighter radar only looks ±60° ahead.
  2. Share. Linked units share every track over the data link. A linked fighter can keep its radar OFF (hollow icon) and still shoot using the AEW&C's track. Its target gets no launch warning.
  3. Shoot. Missiles slow down as they fly. Long shots at a target that turns away usually fail. The inner ring around a selected jet is a rough no-escape range. Missiles like PL-15E can be guided by the data link after launch. Meteor, AIM-120, R-77 and MICA need the shooter's own radar until their seeker turns on.
  4. Support. After firing a radar-guided missile, your jet "cranks", turning 50° off to keep the target in its radar. If it turns away too early, the missile goes blind.
  5. Survive. When warned, jets turn away (drag), jam and drop chaff. Radar emissions give you away: enemies detect radars further than radars detect jets.
YOUR JOB: THREE MISSIONS
  • Tap one of your air bases (□ with a number), choose how many aircraft to send, and pick a mission. Or tap an aircraft that is already flying and give it a new one.
  • CHASE: tap an enemy aircraft. Each fighter carries 4 air-to-air missiles and fires 2 per enemy. An aircraft goes down after 2 hits (1 hit damages it). Your jets hunt it down or chase it back into its own airspace, and they can be shot down too.
  • ESCORT: tap one of your ground-attack flights. The escort flies ahead of it and fights off interceptors. A striker waiting for an escort holds in safe airspace until the escort catches up.
  • GROUND ATTACK: tap an enemy site, radar, SAM or air base, then choose 0, 1 or 2 escorts from the same base. Each striker carries 3 air-to-surface weapons and 2 short-range missiles for self-defence. A target is destroyed after 3 hits. The striker presses in as close as it can: closer shots hit more often and spend less time exposed to enemy fighters and ground defences. If enemy fighters close in, it fires early from further out, so let your escorts clear the way. Drone bases send one-way drones.
  • Shortcut: with a flight selected, tap an enemy aircraft to chase it, or a target to attack it.
  • ⚠ STRIKER INBOUND: when an enemy strike jet is detected it gets a pulsing red ring and a line to its likely target, and the log warns you (again under 100 km). That's your cue to send a CHASE.
  • The AI flies everything else: it dodges missiles, manages radar, goes home at low fuel, refuels and re-arms. AEW&C aircraft fly themselves, and ready fighters scramble on their own when enemies come near their base.
THE RULES
  • Limits: key weapons (Meteor, SCALP, BrahMos, PL-15, CM-400) are limited. Fuel is limited. Only 24 Indian or 20 Pakistani aircraft can fly at once.
  • Air bases: hits close a runway at 60% damage and can destroy jets parked there.
  • Fog of war: kills are only claimed until the debrief reveals the truth.
  • Escalation: every strike and kill raises it. At 100% both sides lose.
  • Replay: after the night, watch the whole battle from both sides.
MAP CONTROLS
  • Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. Speed buttons are at the top. On a keyboard: Space pauses, 1–5 set speed, Esc cancels.
  • ▤ lists your missions. ≡ is the event log.
SYMBOLS
  • Each jet is drawn on its own (delta wing or swept wing by type), and a flight flies in formation · filled: radar ON · hollow: radar silent · amber jet: damaged (one more hit downs it) · dashed: passive (emission-only) track · ◇ target site · □ airbase (number = jets ready) · ▣ SAM site · ✕ drones · ⊕ red reticle: your priority target · amber ring: asset to defend
  • Red line from a missile to your jet: it can still reach you. Grey dashed line: it is losing energy and will fall short if you keep running. The label shows its Mach number and distance.
