KILLCHAIN
An air-war simulation inspired by the India–Pakistan air war of 6–10 May 2025
LIVE: AI vs AI battle running behind this menu

A simplified, unofficial game. Performance figures are open-source estimates; results come from the simulation each time you play, not from either side's claims. Not a historical record.

HOW TO PLAY
  1. Detect. Radar finds enemies. AEW&C aircraft (the circles with a bar) and ground radars see furthest. Fighter radar only looks ±60° ahead.
  2. Share. Linked units share every track over the data link. A linked fighter can keep its radar OFF (hollow icon) and still shoot using the AEW&C's track. Its target gets no launch warning.
  3. Shoot. Missiles slow down as they fly. Long shots at a target that turns away usually fail. The inner ring around a selected jet is a rough no-escape range. Missiles like PL-15E can be guided by the data link after launch. Meteor, AIM-120, R-77 and MICA need the shooter's own radar until their seeker turns on.
  4. Support. After firing a radar-guided missile, your jet "cranks", turning 50° off to keep the target in its radar. If it turns away too early, the missile goes blind.
  5. Survive. When warned, jets turn away (drag), jam and drop chaff. Radar emissions give you away: enemies detect radars further than radars detect jets.
RUNNING THE WAR
  • Air bases. Every jet starts on the ground. Tap a base (□ with a number) to see its aircraft, pick a loadout (AIR for missiles, STRIKE for ground attack) and DEPLOY a flight or SCRAMBLE ALL.
  • Limits. Weapons come from a national stock. Fuel is limited: low fuel means BINGO and the flight goes home. Landing refuels and re-arms (about 7 minutes). Only so many jets can be airborne at once (India 24, Pakistan 20).
  • Strikes. Select a strike flight or drones, then tap an enemy base, SAM site, radar or target. Hits on a base close its runway at 60% damage and can destroy jets parked there. Jets heading home divert elsewhere.
  • Drones. Harop hunts radars by their emissions (turn a SAM's radar OFF to hide it). Swarm drones are cheap: they soak up expensive SAM missiles.
  • EW. LINK JAM cuts the enemy's data link, so silent shots don't work. BARRAGE blinds enemy radars over an area. DECOYS send fake fighters that draw missiles. Each has a cooldown.
  • Decisions. Cards pop up at key moments, with a 10-second timer.
  • Fog of war. Kills are only claimed until the debrief reveals the truth.
  • Escalation. Every strike and kill raises escalation. At 100% the crisis spirals and both sides lose.
  • Replay. After the night, watch the whole battle from both sides.
CONTROLS
  • Tap your flight to select it. Tap the map to send it there (it orbits on arrival).
  • With a flight selected, tap an enemy track to attack it, or tap an enemy base, SAM, radar or target to strike it.
  • Tap an airbase to deploy aircraft. RTB sends a flight home to refuel and re-arm.
  • Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. Speed buttons are at the top. On a keyboard: Space pauses, 1–5 set speed, Esc deselects.
  • ROE FREE: the flight fires on its own at good-quality targets. HOLD: it only fires when you order an attack.
  • BREAK (B): turn tail and run at full speed, so the missile burns its energy chasing you. Best against long shots.
  • NOTCH (N): fly at 90° to the missile. Doppler radars and radar seekers lose a target that is neither closing nor opening. Best against a close, fast missile you can't outrun.
  • Jets defend automatically when warned. BREAK and NOTCH let you choose the move yourself, or act early, before any warning. Tap again to hand control back.
  • ⚠ in the top bar pauses the game whenever one of your jets gets a missile warning.
SYMBOLS
  • ▲ filled: radar ON · △ hollow: radar silent · dashed: passive (emission-only) track · ◇ target site · □ airbase (number = jets ready) · ▣ SAM site · ✕ drones · ⊕ red reticle: your priority target · amber ring: asset to defend
  • Red line from a missile to your jet: it can still reach you. Grey dashed line: it is losing energy and will fall short if you keep running. The label shows its Mach number and distance.
